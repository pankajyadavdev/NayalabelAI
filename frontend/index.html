Statutory Compliance Rules

Legal Metrology Act, 2009 & Packaged Commodities Rules 2011 (Amended to 2026)

Rule 6(1)(a)

Manufacturer Name & Address with PIN

Complete physical address of manufacturer, packer, or importer with mandatory 6-digit postal PIN code.

Rule 6(1)(b)

Generic / Common Name

Unambiguous generic or common commodity identity name conspicuous on principal display panel.

Rule 6(1)(c) & Rule 11/12

Net Quantity & Standard Metric Units

Legal SI metric symbols only ('g', 'kg', 'ml', 'l', 'N'). No pluralization ('gms', 'kgs') and no trailing punctuation ('g.').

Rule 6(1)(d)

Month and Year of Manufacture / Packing

Month and year of manufacture or packaging, plus Best Before / Expiry declaration for perishable goods.

Rule 6(1)(e) & Rule 6(1)(ea)

Maximum Retail Price (MRP) & Unit Sale Price

MRP in Indian Rupees (₹) with mandatory phrase 'inclusive of all taxes'. Unit Sale Price (USP) per g/kg/ml/l mandatory for > 1 unit.

Rule 6(1)(f)

Consumer Grievance Redressal

Name, complete address, telephone helpline number, and email ID of person or grievance officer.
